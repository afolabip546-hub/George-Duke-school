Our History

Growing an enduring educational community in Ibadan.

The story of George & Duke Institutions began in 1995 with the founding of George & Duke Nursery and Elementary School, born out of a desire to provide children with a firm moral and academic bedrock.

Building upon that initial success, George & Duke International College was established in 2000 in Felele to deliver rigorous secondary education. By 2005, the institution expanded with expansive, modern facilities at Odo-Ona Elewe, catering to both day and boarding students across college and advanced level programmes.

Today, George & Duke encompasses three vibrant academic arms: Nursery & Elementary, International College, and the Advanced Level Programme. Throughout our growth, our guiding philosophy remains constant: uniting academic excellence with sound moral character and practical preparation for tomorrow's world.
