Life Beyond the Classroom

Nurturing passions, leadership & lifelong friendships.

At George & Duke, education extends far beyond textbooks. Discover a vibrant school community offering safe boarding, competitive athletics, creative expression, and diverse student societies.

A Rich School Experience

Growing together in confidence, camaraderie, and purpose.

Student life at George & Duke Institutions is deliberately structured to foster holistic development. We believe every child possesses unique talents waiting to be awakened.

Through our diverse co-curricular activities, residential boarding life, inter-house competitions, and creative arts festivals, students learn vital life skills: collaboration, resilience, self-discipline, and empathy.

Residential Life

A supportive home-away-from-home environment.

Our modern boarding house at the Odo-Ona Elewe campus offers a secure, well-structured, and caring residential community for secondary college and A-Level students.

Supervised by dedicated housemasters and housemistresses, boarders benefit from balanced daily routines, supervised evening prep sessions, wholesome nutrition, health care, and weekend recreational activities that build lasting independence and lifelong friendships.

Athletics & Fitness

Sport, healthy competition, and teamwork.

Physical fitness and sportsmanship are vital components of the George & Duke student experience.

Team Sports

Field & Court Games

Students compete actively in football, basketball, volleyball, table tennis, badminton, and relay racing, developing stamina, tactical thinking, and cooperative teamwork under professional coaching.

Distinction

Specialized Sporting Pursuits

Beyond conventional sports, George & Duke introduces students to unique sporting disciplines including horse riding, polo clinics, and swimming training, broadening physical horizons and building confidence.

Our annual Inter-House Sports Festival brings the entire school community together in spirited celebration of athletic achievement, perseverance, and house pride.

Creative & Performing Arts

Creativity, musical performance, and artistic expression.

A rich creative ecosystem giving every student the platform to paint, perform, sing, and express their unique artistic voice.

Visual Arts

Studio Arts & Design

Dedicated art studios where students explore drawing, watercolor, ceramics, sculpture, graphic design, and textile craft, showcasing their work in school exhibitions.

Music & Orchestra

Music, Choir & Dance

Formal vocal training, school choir, wind/brass instruments, piano lessons, and traditional African drumming that culminate in magnificent carol services and recitals.

Performing Arts

Theatre & Dramatic Arts

Stage productions, dramatic monologues, scriptwriting, and public elocution that develop poise, emotional intelligence, and confident stage presence.

Extracurricular Life

Clubs that fuel curiosity and practical skills.

Clubs meet weekly, allowing students to pursue passions outside formal academic syllabi, learn new hobbies, and collaborate across year groups.

Debate & Literary Society

Honing public speaking, persuasive rhetoric, and research skills through inter-school debate tournaments.

Chess & Strategy Club

Developing strategic foresight, spatial reasoning, and intellectual patience through tournament play.

Scouts & Girl Guides

Outdoor survival skills, first aid training, team building, camping expeditions, and civic duty.

Young Farmers Club

Hands-on agronomy, vegetable gardening, sustainable farm management, and agro-business insights.

STEM & Innovation Society

Robotics workshops, scientific model construction, coding challenges, and science fair preparation.

Cultural Troupe & Talent Shows

Celebrations of Nigerian cultural heritage, traditional choreography, and student talent showcases.

Character & Leadership

Equipping students for principled leadership.

Leadership at George & Duke is learned through responsibility, service, and ethical accountability.

Governance

Prefect Body & Student Council

Elected and appointed student prefects serve as liaisons between students and faculty, organizing assemblies, maintaining order, and championing student welfare.

Mentorship

Peer Mentoring Program

Senior students mentor junior peers, providing guidance on study habits, hostel adaptation, and emotional well-being within a family-like school culture.

Social Impact

Community Service Initiatives

Students participate in community clean-up projects, charitable visits, and environmental awareness drives, nurturing social conscience and empathy.
