Visiting Us

Plan Your Campus Visit

Visiting George & Duke is the best way to experience our learning culture and meet our educators.

Guideline 01

Visiting Hours

Official administrative offices and campus tour desks are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Parents are encouraged to book in advance to ensure coordinator availability.

Guideline 02

Campus Security

All visitors are required to sign in at the security gatehouse on arrival with a valid identity document. Our security personnel will issue a visitor's pass and direct you to the front desk.

Guideline 03

Guided Tours

Personalized guided tours are conducted on weekdays between 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM, giving families the opportunity to observe classroom activities, laboratories, hostels, and sports grounds.
