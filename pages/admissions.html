Entry Guidelines

Admission Requirements by Academic Level

Ensure all supporting documents are prepared prior to submitting your formal application.

Early Years & Primary

Nursery & Elementary

  • • Child must be at least 18 months for Crèche, 3 years for Nursery, or 5+ for Primary 1.
  • • Completed George & Duke Application Form.
  • • Photocopy of Birth Certificate or Statutory Declaration of Age.
  • • Up-to-date Child Immunization & Health Record.
  • • 2 recent passport-sized photographs of applicant.
  • • Academic progress report from previous school (if applying for Primary 2–6).
Secondary Level

International College

  • • Candidate must have successfully completed Primary 6 education (for JSS 1 entry).
  • • Completed Application Form with 4 recent passport photographs.
  • • Birth certificate photocopy and medical fitness certificate.
  • • Primary School Leaving Certificate / Testimonial.
  • • Last two terms' academic report cards from previous school.
  • • Satisfactory performance in the George & Duke Entrance Examination (Mathematics & English) and candidate interview.
Pre-University

A-Level Programme

  • • Minimum of 5 credits in relevant subjects at SSCE (WAEC / NECO / IGCSE), including Mathematics and English in one sitting.
  • • Completed A-Level Registration Form and 4 passport photographs.
  • • Official Senior Secondary School Transcript and Testimonial.
  • • Copy of Birth Certificate and National Identification Number (NIN).
  • • Academic consultation and placement interview with the Director of Studies.

How to Apply

Our 4-Step Enrolment Procedure

We make the admissions journey transparent, supportive, and straightforward for families.

Step 01

Obtain the Form

Pick up an application package directly from the administrative offices at either our Felele or Odo-Ona campus, or submit an enquiry online through our contact page.

Step 02

Submit Documentation

Complete the application form and return it to the admissions office alongside required birth certificates, academic records, and passport photographs.

Step 03

Assessment & Interview

Prospective pupils/students sit for our entrance assessment (diagnostic screening for early years; written exam in Math & English for college and A-Levels), followed by a brief parent interview.

Step 04

Offer & Enrolment

Successful applicants receive a formal letter of admission. Secure your child's placement by completing registration fees and receiving our orientation pack.
